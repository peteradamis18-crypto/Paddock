## What to build
- **One engine, one API, cached and queued.** `GET /api/review?id=<telemetry file id | last>` builds the whole review of
  one session from the lap store, the corner set, the measures and the field log, and answers at once from a cache
  (`dash\data\review\<id>.json`, written temp + move); a review not built yet answers `{"state":"working","say":"Reading
  your laps…"}` with HTTP 202 and queues the build as `Jobs.Run("review " + id, …, whenFree)` (one BelowNormal job, never
  on a request thread, never while a session is on: the request answers "working" meanwhile and the page asks again on
  `data.onList('review')` — bump the list on completion with `StateBuilder.BumpLists`). The newest finished session's
  review is built by itself when the sim closes (the same hook W0-3 uses for the session file). "last" = the newest
  finished session from the lap store (one picker, replacing the five). Old routes stay working through the engine until
  wave 6 deletes them.
- **The JSON (contract)**:
  ```
  {"state":"ready","session":{"id","track","config","car","type":"Practice|Qualify|Race|Test","date":ms,"laps":10,"clean":8,
     "best":{"lap":9,"time":93.832},"ref":{"kind":"session|ever","lap":9,"time":93.832,"date":ms,"conditions":"track 31°, 42 L fuel"},
     "result":{"finish":4,"start":7,"field":19,"irating":null}},
   "cards":[{"rank":1,"corner":{"n":5,"label":"T5","name":"","detected":false,"p0":0.31,"pa":0.33,"p1":0.35},"lost":0.31,
             "happened":"You braked 14 m earlier than your best lap","tryThis":"Brake at the 100 m board","sample":"in 6 of 8 clean laps",
             "phase":"brake","clip":{"id":"rec-…","at":12.4}}],
   "plan":"Next time: Turn 5, brake at the 100 m board, check the entry speed",
   "race":null | {"positions":[4,5,5,4,…],"pace":{"mine":94.1,"field":94.8,"cars":19},"corners":[{"n":5,"label":"T5","lost":0.4,"clip":{…}}],"cleanAir":card|null},
   "map":{"key":"roadatlanta-full","id":1,"gain":[…per 1 % of the lap: seconds gained (+) / lost (−) vs the reference…],"marks":[{"n":1,"p":0.33},…]},
   "laps":[{"n":9,"time":93.832,"counted":true,"why":"","sectors":[…],"pb":true}],
   "optimal":{"time":93.4,"from":[{"sector":1,"lap":9,"date":ms}]},
   "note":null | "No full lap yet" | "Only 2 clean laps: the costliest corner, no advice"}
  ```
  `GET /api/review/traces?id&lap&ref` = today's `/analysis/channels` PWB1 (the 1 m grid for both laps: time, speed,
  throttle, brake, steering, gear, lat/lon) plus the corner set in its header: W4-2's chart reads it.
  `GET /api/review/corners?id&lap&ref` → rows `{n,label,detected,brakeM,minSpeed,pickupM,time}` for this lap and the
  reference with the four definitions once (`"defs":{"brakeM":"brake over 10 %",…}`).
  `GET /api/review/progress?car&track` → `{"sessions":[{"date","best","avg5"}],"corners":[{"n","label","lost":[0.31,0.22,0.12],"dates":[…]}],"irating":[{"date","value","estimate":true}]}`.
- **Rules (PLAN 4.4):** the reference is this session's best clean counted lap; under 3 clean laps his best ever at this
  car and track (`ref.kind:"ever"`, with the conditions line: track temp and fuel at the start, stored per session from
  the .ibt `TrackTemp`/`FuelLevel` at the lap's start); cards ranked by seconds lost, at most 3, the sample size, "try
  this" in one line (distance to the brake point rounded to a board: 50/100/150/200 m when the brake metric is the cause,
  else the phase's own words), the plan line from card 1; under 3 clean laps one card with numbers and no advice; a race
  gets the race story from the field log and at most one "clean-air lap" card; corner names "detected" when not Lovely;
  `cornerCache` never caches empties; `LoadCorners` only if the file exists; `Gather` reads the lap-store index, not 40
  files; the wording lint as a pure function `Review.Lint(text)` → the first rule broken or null (no percentages, no
  "mistake"/"error"/"bad", no "you should", ≤ 14 words, a corner and a number on every card); units follow Peter's
  iRacing setting (DisplayUnits from the file's YAML: mph/gal or km/h/L) — the numbers in `happened` use it.
- Nothing heavy on a request thread; the engine's file reads stop on `Jobs.ShouldYield`.
