## Why
Three small things Peter asked for or must do himself: the car photos on the side screens come up out of order;
he has to do a one-time 10-minute Google setup before Paddock can upload to his YouTube channel (the only thing we
cannot do for him); and we want to know whether Coach Dave Delta is what freezes his races.

## What to build

### 1. Photo order (C6)
Find why the car photos are out of order. Two suspects: the side-screen gallery (`dash/src/gallery.cs` lines
630-680: files from the photo folder and `mine` concatenated and sorted by name, then shown in pairs) and the car
pictures strip on HOME / Analysis (`dash/src/cars.cs`, `dash/www/v2/carpics.js`). Read `docs/rework/evidence/01-current-screens.md`
and `docs/REWORK-LIST.md` for Peter's words ("car photos out of order"). Decide from the code and the files on
disk (list the photo folders and their names/dates in your report: the folders are named in `gallery.cs`; never
delete or move a photo). The rule from PLAN §4.7: the newest set first, a stable order within a set, pairs that
belong together stay together (`docs/rework/evidence/01` and memory say pairs change together). Fix the one that is
wrong, in the smallest change; keep the 120 s pairs, one caption, no branding.
Test `tests\photo-order-test.ps1`: a temp folder with three "sets" (by date prefix or subfolder, whatever the real
folders use) in random creation order → the order function returns newest set first, same order on two calls,
pairs intact. Pure function: move the ordering into a small static method so the test can compile it alone.

### 2. `docs/YOUTUBE-SETUP.md`: the one-time Google setup, click by click, for Peter
Plain English, numbered, about 10 minutes, nothing to decide. Cover, in order: open Google Cloud console with his
Google account, make a project called Paddock, enable "YouTube Data API v3", the OAuth consent screen (External,
app name Paddock, his email, scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload`, then **Publish app** so it is
"In production": in Testing the sign-in expires after 7 days and he would have to do it again), create an OAuth
client of type **Desktop app**, download the JSON, save it as `C:\Users\peter\iRacingDeck\dash\yt\client.json`
(add `dash/yt/` to `.gitignore` now: the repo is public), then what Paddock does on the first SHARE ON YOUTUBE (a
browser tab opens, he picks his account, clicks Allow, the tab says done). Also one short section "If Google shows a
warning that the app is not verified": click Advanced, then Go to Paddock (it is his own app). One line on the
daily limit (about 100 uploads a day; we will never get near it).
Check the console's current wording by fetching Google's own pages (`https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/guides/auth/installed-apps`,
`https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/6158849`, `https://developers.google.com/youtube/registering_an_application`):
button names change; write what the pages say today and date the guide. No screenshots needed. Never put any id,
secret or token in the doc or anywhere in the repo.
Test `tests\youtube-guide-test.ps1`: the file exists, every step is numbered, it names the exact `client.json` path,
`.gitignore` has `dash/yt/`, and the doc contains no string that looks like a client id or secret.

### 3. `docs/rework/DELTA-TEST.md`: the Coach Dave Delta three-race protocol
One page the lead follows one evening when the rig is free and Peter is away. Same 20-minute AI race three times
(same car, track, AI count and weather as the 12 Nov race: Toyota GR86, Road Atlanta full), with Delta: (a) not
running, (b) running with its video capture off, (c) running with capture on. What is recorded each time: the .ibt
(FrameRate, CpuUsageFG, GpuUsage per second), the black box rows (W0-9 builds it; until then, the Performance page
report and `dash/data/perf/*`), Delta's CPU % and private bytes every second (`Get-Counter` or the existing
sampler), disk queue length. How to decide: average fps, the worst second, the number of seconds under 60 fps, and
one sentence per run; the verdict goes on RIG in Peter's words ("Coach Dave Delta cost you 9 fps and one freeze at
lap 9; leave it off for races" or "Delta made no difference"). How the lead starts an AI race without touching the
iRacing UI by hand (`keys\airace*.exe` / `irdeck-cli airace start`, see `src/testdrive.cs`) and the guard rules
(`Assert-RigFree -NeedAway`). Keep it to the facts; no promises about the result.
