# W1-3 — `Cuts`: trim the out lap, chapters, title templates

Wave 1 (PLAN §4.3 "Share to YouTube" step 2 and "Title templates", evidence 14 §2 B and C, evidence 04 §2 and §6 point 10).
Builder: Sonnet, then a review. Rules: `docs/rework/cards/README.md`. Runs in parallel with W1-1 (`library.cs`,
`recorder.cs`, `vault.cs`, `irdash.cs`) and W1-2 (`youtube.cs`): touch only `dash\src\cuts.cs` (already in
`dash\build.ps1`'s `$src`; replace the stub) and your tests. Pure rules plus one ffmpeg run: no screen, no rig, no routes.
W1-4 calls it: `Cuts.Plan` → `Cuts.Title` / `Cuts.Description` → `Cuts.Run` → `YouTube.Enqueue`.

## Inputs (what exists)
A finished session = `vault\sessions\<id>.mp4` (HEVC, keyframe about every 1 s, no B-frames, AAC) and its sidecar
`vault\sessions\<id>.json` (schema 2, written by `SessJson` in `dash\src\recorder.cs` ~1585): `track, config, car,
sessionType, startMs, durationSec, laps:[{n, offset, time, clean, counted?, pb?, pit?}], incidents:[{offset, count}]`
(`offset` = seconds from the start of the video; `pit` arrives with W1-1, older sidecars have none). Read it with
`System.Web.Script.Serialization.JavaScriptSerializer` (already referenced) — do not write a JSON parser.
ffmpeg and ffprobe: `vault\bin\ffmpeg.exe`, `vault\bin\ffprobe.exe` (`Recorder.FfmpegExe`; in `irdash --test` the
recorder's mock stands in: in your test call your own `ffmpegPath` argument).

## What to build (`dash\src\cuts.cs`, static class `Cuts`, C# 5)
- `CutPlan Plan(string sidecarJson, double durationSec)`: `StartSec` = lap 1's `offset` − 3 (never below 0) when the
  session is a race with a lap 1 row, else 0 (evidence 14 B: "the end of the out lap minus 3 s"; the cut lands on the
  keyframe at or before it, so the picture starts at most 1 s earlier still, never late); `EndSec` = the last lap's
  `offset + time` + 20, capped at `durationSec`; with no lap rows the whole file. `Chapters`: a list of `(Sec, Label)`
  relative to `StartSec`: always `0:00 Start` first; then every incident ("Incident +2x"), every pit lap ("Pit stop",
  at that lap's offset), the counted best lap ("Best lap 1:33.832 (lap 9)", the quickest row with `counted` true, else
  `clean` true, else none), "Finish" at the last lap's end; drop any chapter closer than 10 s to the one before it (keep
  the earlier one); fewer than 3 → add "Halfway" at the middle (YouTube needs three for the chapter bar); never a chapter
  after `EndSec`. `Why` says the plan in one sentence for the log ("out lap off (lap 1 at 95.2 s), 20 s after the finish, 6 chapters").
- `string Title(int template, Facts f)`: the five templates, picked once in settings (W1-2's `[youtube] template`):
  1 `{Car} at {Track} · iRacing {Series} · {Date}` · 2 `P{Finish} from P{Start} · {Car} at {Track}` ·
  3 `{Series}: {Track} in the {Car} · P{Finish}` · 4 `{Track} {Car} full race · {Date}` · 5 `Full race: {Series} at {Track} ({Car})`.
  `Facts` = Car, Track, Config, Series, Date (DateTime), Finish, Start (ints, 0 = unknown). `{Series}` only when known
  (the segment and its separator vanish: template 3 without a series becomes `{Track} in the {Car} · P{Finish}`);
  P-fields unknown → that segment goes too (template 2 falls back to `{Car} at {Track} · {Date}`). The date is
  "13 Nov 2026". Under 70 characters: shorten in this order — drop the config, drop " · {Date}", cut the series, then
  the track — never cut a word in half. Never a `{` left in the output.
- `string Description(CutPlan p, Facts f)`: one line of facts ("Toyota GR86 at Road Atlanta, 13 Nov 2026 · 10 laps ·
  best 1:33.832"), a blank line, the chapters one per line as `m:ss Label` (`h:mm:ss` past an hour), a blank line,
  "Recorded with Paddock". The first chapter line must be exactly `0:00 Start`.
- `string FfmpegArgs(string inPath, string outPath, CutPlan p)`: evidence 14 B's command — `-hide_banner -y -ss <start>
  -to <end> -i "<in>" -c copy -map 0:v:0 -map 0:a:0 -tag:v hvc1 -movflags +faststart -avoid_negative_ts make_zero "<out>"`
  (`-ss` before `-i`: it seeks to the keyframe, audio stays in sync). Invariant culture for every number.
- `string Run(string ffmpegPath, string ffprobePath, string inPath, string outDir, CutPlan p, out string outPath)`:
  runs ffmpeg through `Proc` at BelowNormal (as the recorder's work thread does), output `<outDir>\<id>-cut.mp4`,
  refuses when less than 10 GB is free (evidence 14 G) with "Not enough space for the cut (needs 10 GB free)", then
  checks with ffprobe that the output's duration is within 2 s of `EndSec − StartSec`; returns "ok" or the reason in
  plain words. The caller (W1-4) wraps it in `Jobs.Run(whenFree)`; `Run` itself must not touch `Jobs`.
