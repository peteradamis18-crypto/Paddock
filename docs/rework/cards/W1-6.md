## How (decided; the sync problem is solved in the browser, not with dshow)
- **Player** = Chrome/Edge in app mode, the way irdash opens the dashboard (`irdash.cs` ~3230: `BrowserExe()`,
  `--user-data-dir`, `--window-position/size`, `--app=`): a second profile folder `dash\player-profile`, placed on the
  **Windows primary screen** (`Screen.PrimaryScreen.Bounds` = the middle race screen, CLAUDE.md "The rig"),
  `--start-fullscreen --autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required --use-fake-ui-for-media-stream` (the last one grants
  the microphone without a prompt and picks Windows' default recording device: Peter clicks nothing). Closing = end
  the browser process whose command line holds `player.html` (never the dashboard's).
- **`player.html`** (`<video>` of `/api/library/video?id=…&cut=1`, served with Range support like `ServeMp4`): on load it
  checks `canPlayType('video/mp4; codecs="hvc1.1.6.L153.B0"')` and, if unsupported, shows "This PC cannot play the video
  in Paddock's player" and posts it (LIVE check: the rig's Chrome with the RTX 4080 is expected to play HEVC). Then
  `getUserMedia({audio:{echoCancellation:false, noiseSuppression:true}})`, a `MediaRecorder` (audio/webm;codecs=opus),
  `recorder.start()` and `video.play()` in the same tick; on the recorder's `start` event note `t0 = performance.now()`;
  on the video's `playing` event note `t1` and `video.currentTime` → **`D = currentTime − (t1 − t0)/1000`** = the video
  time at which the voice track's first sample belongs (negative = the voice started after the video did: the mix delays
  the voice by −D). A level meter from an `AnalyserNode`; silence = RMS under −50 dB for 5 s. STOP (the key, Esc, the
  deck via `/api/talk/state` polled once a second only while talking): `recorder.stop()`, `POST /api/talk/voice?id=…`
  with the blob and `D`, `secs`, `mic` (device label), `silent` as query fields (X-Paddock header via `data.fetch`);
  the page then says "Mixing… you can close this" and irdash closes it. Seeking and pausing are disabled while talking (v1).
- **`Commentary`** (`commentary.cs`): `Start(id)` (from `racetalk`, GuardedDo — it opens a window and plays sound on his
  screens): refuses while `RigState.SessionOn`; makes the cut if missing (`Cuts.Plan/Run` through `Jobs.Run(whenFree)`,
  the card's state `talk.kind = preparing` meanwhile), then opens the player; `Stop(id)` (from `racetalkstop`, NOT guarded:
  it only ends things) closes the player and waits for the voice POST (up to 10 s); `OnVoice(id, blob, D, …)` writes
  `vault\commentary\<id>-voice.webm` + a small `.json` (D, secs, mic, when) and queues the mix (`Jobs.Run(whenFree)`):
  evidence 14 F's command with the voice aligned by `D` (`-ss D` on the voice input when D > 0: its first D seconds
  were spoken before the video started; `-itsoffset −D` on the voice input when D < 0), `sidechaincompress=threshold=0.04:ratio=8:attack=20:release=400`,
  `amix=inputs=2:duration=first:normalize=0`, `alimiter=limit=0.95`, `-c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -tag:v hvc1
  -movflags +faststart` → `vault\commentary\<id>-commentary.mp4`; verified with ffprobe (duration within 1 s of the cut,
  two streams) → `talk.kind = ready`; the voice file stays until the mix is verified. A `SessionOn` turning true while
  talking = `Stop` (the state says "stopped: a session started; what you said is kept"). Everything logged in one line each.
- **Share**: `raceshare a=<id>:c` (W1-4's chain, the `:c` suffix meaning the commentary file) → `YouTube.Enqueue(id + ":c",
  commentary.mp4, Cuts.Title(...) + " (with commentary)", same description, privacy, the race's thumb)`; title stays
  under 70 characters (shorten the title first, never the suffix). W1-2's `uploads.json` keys by that id, so the
  "never twice" rule holds for the second video on its own.
- **State** (in `/api/library` per race): `"talk":{"kind":"none|preparing|talking|mixing|ready|shared|failed","sec":192,
  "mic":"Arctis Nova 7P","silent":false,"url":"https://youtu.be/…","error":"…"}` (`shared`/`url` from W1-2's
  `StateJson(id + ":c")`); `/api/talk/state?id=` for the player (`{"stop":true}` once STOP was pressed on the deck).
- **Page** (`races.js`): TALK OVER IT enabled; while `talking`/`mixing` the open card shows a bar "Talking 03:12 · STOP" /
  "Mixing your commentary…"; `ready` shows the second state line with SHARE; the silent warning in words.
- **Deck** (`plugin.html`, the RACES screen): while any race is `preparing|talking|mixing` the whole screen is the talking
  view — "TALKING 03:12" across 4 keys, the mic name under it, STOP red 3×2 (`racetalkstop`), the silent warning in
  amber when `silent`, EXIT at 7,3 (EXIT does not stop the talk; STOP does). The open view gets TALK OVER IT (gold,
  `racetalk`) and, when `ready`, "SHARE COMMENTARY" (`raceshare a=<id>:c`).
