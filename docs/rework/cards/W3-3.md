## Data made true (server)
- **His series and the next race** (`official.cs`): today "his series" = favourites only and `favourites.ini` does not
  exist, so `next` is null and nothing is filtered (scout). Make it: favourites ∪ the series of his last 10 races,
  found by matching each race's (car, track, week) from the session index / `career-cache.tsv` / the recorder's
  sessions to the schedule's rows for that week (`officialcore.cs`); if no series matches, the fallback PLAN names:
  the next session he has a car and track for. `/official` gains `"mine":[…]` (his series keys) and `next` becomes the
  next race of his series in local time (`localStart`, `countdownSeconds` exist). Log one line at start-up: "official:
  Peter's series: GR Cup, …" so the lead can confirm §9 C1 live.
- **Finish and start positions** (`recorder.cs` + `library.cs`): at session end the recorder writes `"start"` and
  `"finish"` into the sidecar from the SDK's session results (the `session` part's `SessionInfo.Sessions[].ResultsPositions`
  for his CarIdx: `Position` at the end; the start from the first results seen after the green / the qualifying result;
  say what you could get) and `/api/library` races carry `finish`/`start` (0 = unknown). W1-4's titles then fill
  `P{Finish} from P{Start}` for new races (`Cuts.Facts` via `library.cs FactsOf`): one line.
- The after-drive card reads the newest race from `/api/library` once `data.onList('races')` fires after the drive.
