## What exists

(6 KB, z 1000) plays a gold "Pre-race systems" screen for 12–14 s once per boot and on `state.showcase.seq` (the deck

PLAN says local. `design.css` tokens: `--gold #C9A24B`, `--ink`, `--muted`, the type scale `--t-hero 240 … --t-label 20`.

## The clock (`app/lib/clock.js`, ES module)
`Clock.mount(el, { size: 720, ring: 0..1|null, readout: string|null })` draws a 720 px analog dial on a canvas:
warm-white indices (12 ticks, the quarters longer), no numerals, no brand, hour and minute hands in `--ink`, the
second hand in `--gold` sweeping smoothly (requestAnimationFrame while the page is visible, nothing when hidden);
`Clock.set({ ring, readout })` updates the thin gold ring (closes clockwise from 12 as `ring` goes 0→1; hidden when
null) and the one readout under 12 o'clock (56 px, `--t-title`, e.g. "NEXT GREEN FLAG 1:42:09"); `Clock.unmount()`.
Local time always. `Clock.now` is replaceable (tests pass a fixed clock). Draw cost: under 1 ms a frame (measure).

## The start-up (`app/pages/startup.js`)
`Startup.play(el, { sound: true, quiet: isQuietHours(), skipOn: ['keydown','pointerdown'] }) → Promise<'done'|'skipped'>`:
black; five gold lights appear left to right, one per second, each with a soft low tone (Web Audio: a short sine at
~110 Hz with a 30 ms attack and a 400 ms decay, −18 dBFS: generated, no files); one beat of silence (1 s); lights out;
the dial (`Clock`) fades up over 1.2 s with the second hand already sweeping, and the Promise resolves so the shell
can fade HOME in and the side screens their photo (the gallery is irdash's: nothing to do here beyond resolving).
Total 7 s. Any key or pointer skips to the end at once. `quiet` = 23:00–07:00 local: no tones. Plays once per
dashboard boot and when `state.showcase.seq` rises while not driving (the same trigger intro.js uses: read it there);
never while driving; `?intro=1` forces it, `?hold=1` freezes it at the fifth light (for pictures). `intro.js` /
`intro.css` are retired (the lead deletes them when W3-1 and W3-3 land; say what else referenced them).
