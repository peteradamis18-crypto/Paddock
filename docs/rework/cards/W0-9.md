## Why
Peter's worst freeze (12 Nov 09:28 Road Atlanta, the race he won, laps 9-10, 112 → 24 fps for about 10 s) has
no known cause: the 5 s sampler keeps only a per-drive average, the Performance verdict said "160 fps held" about
an older file, ignored the worst second and named no track or date, and the doctor reported "all fine" from an
older session. After any freeze Paddock must say one sentence with the lap, the time and the likely cause.

## What to build

1. **The black box** (new `dash/src/blackbox.cs`): while `RigState.SimRunning`, one row per second to
   `dash\data\blackbox\<session-start>.tsv` (about 1.2 KB/s, 4 MB per 20-minute race, kept 8 weeks, pruned as a
   `Jobs` job when W0-3 has merged, else on the existing worker between sessions). Columns: time, session time,
   lap, lap position, speed, `FrameRate`, `CpuUsageFG`, `CpuUsageBG`, `GpuUsage`, `MemPageFaultSec` (from the SDK
   frame already read), total CPU %, the 3 busiest programs with their CPU share (the existing sampler's reads,
   per-process every 5 s unless a dip is on), disk queue length and write latency for the drive holding the vault
   and the sim (performance counters), and Paddock's own events in the last second (lap crossing, cut start/end,
   file save, scan, job start/end: the modules call `BlackBox.Event("cut start")`). Cost under 1 % of one core at
   BelowNormal, nothing on the render or UI thread; measure it and print the number in the test. NVML (GPU
   clocks, temperature, throttle reason) is wave 5 (W5-3): leave a clearly named stub column set.
2. **Fast mode:** when `FrameRate` falls 25 % or more against the last 5 s average, write a row every 100 ms for
   15 s and snapshot the 3 busiest programs plus GPU and disk state at the moment of the dip (one `snapshot` line
   in the same file). Then back to 1 s.
3. **The cause rules** (one function, pure, tested): GPU at 95 %+ → "graphics card"; one program at 3× its own
   average over the session → "that program (name)"; a cut, save or scan in the 3 s before the dip → "Paddock
   (what)"; page faults over 1000/s → "memory"; nothing unusual → "inside the sim (likely a graphics hitch)".
   Output: one plain sentence in Peter's words with the lap, the place on the lap (start/finish, or the corner
   name if `corners` knows it), the length of the freeze and the cause: "Lap 9, start/finish: the picture froze for
   4 s. Likely cause: Coach Dave Delta (3× its usual CPU)."
4. **Doctor and Performance fixes (N3, 09 §1b):** the verdict is about the session just driven or says "still being
   processed" (never names an older file); the sentence carries track and date; it names a freeze whenever the
   worst second is under half the average, with the lap and time; "held" is measured against the fps cap from the
   sim's own setting (one `SimSettings` reader replacing the three `[Graphics Options]` parsers), not the highest
   sample; `Start` comes from the header time, then the file name, never `CreationTimeUtc`; `/perf/status` caches
   its file list (no 590-file listing per call); no doctor run while `SessionOn` (W0-2 added the guard: show the
   cached result with its time); one owner for starting CrewChief (the doctor asks `SpotterGuard`, or the other
   way round: pick one, delete the other path); `PerfTest` / `SetNamed` behind the test hook.
5. **Where it shows (v1, minimal):** the Performance page's top line and the Rig Doctor card show the black box
   sentence for the last session when something happened, "No freezes in your last race (avg 104 fps)" when not.
   RIG's full design is wave 5; do not restyle.
