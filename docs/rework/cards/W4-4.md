## The screen (1920×1080 under the shell's header; nothing under 24 px; readouts 32 px)
- **Top-left:** `Map.mount` with the cursor dot (`Map.cursor`) following the chart's cursor; coloured by the gain.
- **Lap picker:** two rows of lap chips: THIS LAP (the session's laps from `/api/review` `laps[]`, counted ones bright,
  others dim with their `why` on the chip, the best purple) and REFERENCE (the same laps plus "YOUR BEST HERE" when
  `ref.kind` is `ever`); a press loads `/api/review/traces?id&lap&ref` and `/api/review/corners` (`data.json`, no polling).
- **The stack:** `Chart.mount` with rows delta-T (gold), speed, throttle + brake, steering, gear, on one distance axis
  with the corner bands; the readout column always visible (both laps' values at the cursor, 32 px).
- **The corner table** under the stack: corner · brake point (m) · minimum speed · throttle-on point (m) · time, each
  "this lap vs reference" (two columns + the difference coloured gain/loss), with the four definitions shown once
  under the table ("brake point = brake over 10 %", "throttle-on = throttle over 90 % after the apex", "minimum speed
  = the slowest point", "time = turn-in to exit"); a press on a row = `Chart.zoomCorner(n)` + `Map.cursor`.
- **Theoretical best:** "Theoretical best 1:33.4 from laps 3, 7, 9 (6 Nov – 13 Nov)" from `optimal` with its date spread.
- **Zoom and the deck:** wheel zoom around the cursor; `onDeck(cmd)` handles the deck's `wb-*` commands (next/prev corner,
  whole lap, cursor step) through `Chart.zoomCorner/zoomLap/setCursor` (the page listens to the `workbench` state part
  the deck already sends; no POST on a timer: remove the `/workbench/cmd` poller pattern); `opts.corner` from SHOW ME
  zooms to that corner at mount.
- **States:** two laps loaded; this lap only (no reference: the delta row says "no reference lap"); no complete lap
  ("No full lap yet": the picker empty, the note); working; offline.
- Units from `session`/the file (mph/km/h) as W4-1 gives them; no mouse-only tooltips.
