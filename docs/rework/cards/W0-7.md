## What to build

1. **`dash/www/app/design.css`**, the only `:root` in the new tree:
   - Colour tokens (at most 16, these names): `--bg` #050505, `--panel` #0E0E0E, `--line` (hairline on panel),
     `--ink` #F2EEE4, `--muted` (ink at 55 %), `--dim` (ink at 30 %), `--gold`, `--gold-ink` (text on gold),
     `--gold-dim`, `--gain` (green), `--loss` (red), `--purple` (session best), `--amber`, `--cyan`, `--danger`
     (the delete red), `--focus`. Flag colours stay out of it (the flag rule: real flag colours only when a real
     flag shows; `flaglow.js` keeps its own).
   - Two golds: `:root` = today's bright `#f2c230`; `:root[data-gold="soft"]` = broadcast `#C9A24B`, each with its
     own `--gold-ink` and `--gold-dim`. Nothing else differs.
   - Type scale as tokens: `--t-hero` 240px, `--t-display` 96px, `--t-title` 56px, `--t-read` 36px, `--t-body` 24px
     (the seat minimum), `--t-label` 20px (the lean-in minimum; caps, +8 % tracking). Family: Bahnschrift (installed
     on this PC; today's pages use it), falling back to "Segoe UI Variable Display", "Segoe UI". Tabular numerals
     (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) everywhere. No weight under 400, no hairline under 2 px.
   - One button (`.btn`, at least 64 px tall, gold / plain / danger variants, 0.7 s press flash), one header
     (`.hdr`: title left, clock or state right, one CLOSE), one table, one empty state (`.empty`: one sentence, one
     action), one banner line (`.banner`: offline, kids, key message). Motion: 200 ms state changes, 400-700 ms
     entrances, slow-in fast-out, nothing moves more than 24 px, no bounce.
   - A short comment at the top: what the tokens mean and the two rules (no hex outside this file; seat ≥ 24 px).
2. **`dash/www/app/lib/format.js`** (plain script, also usable as an ES module later): `lap(s)` → `1:33.832`
