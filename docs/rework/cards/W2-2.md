## The screen (the 1920×1080 dashboard monitor above the race screens; black; 24 px margin; nothing under 24 px; one
`design.css`; no new hex, no new font size: the type-scale tokens win over the pixel sizes PLAN names)
Zones and channels exactly as PLAN §4.2's table: **A** top strip (position / class position, lap and to-go or time
left, incident chip amber within 3 of the limit, session clock); **B** gaps AHEAD / BEHIND with number, short name,
green closing / red losing, trend arrow from the last 3 laps; **C** fuel ("FUEL +1.8 LAPS" green / "−0.6 LAPS" red,
laps of fuel and laps to go, the pit-window bar of 24 px cells coloured white/red/orange/green, "SAVE 0.12 L/LAP to
gain a lap" when `fuel.save`, "FUEL: measuring (2 of 3 laps)" before); **D** last lap (purple when `lastIsBest`),
best, the delta bar 912×48 from `lap.delta` hidden when `deltaOk` is false; **E** standings: 11 rows × 80 px, class
leader pinned, 4 ahead, YOU (gold, 1.3×), 4 behind; columns class position, number on a class-colour band, short name,
interval to the car in front, PIT chip (grey row on pit road); class header rows only when multiclass. Names, numbers
and classes come from the state's `session` part (DriverInfo.Drivers by CarIdx); order and intervals from the stream's
`CarIdxClassPosition`, `CarIdxF2Time`, `CarIdxEstTime`, `CarIdxOnPitRoad`; everything else from the `race` part.
**States** (each one a picture): green; **caution** (strip CAUTION amber for the whole caution, B = gap to leader,
C = "PIT NOW? needs 14 L, have 22 L", E = gap to leader + PIT chips, D faded); **on pit road** (B and D collapse, C =
limiter state and fuel in tank, E = the ghost row "YOU REJOIN P7" only when `pit.rejoin` is not null); **practice /
qualifying** (E = this session's lap list: lap, time, delta to best; B = "best 1:33.832 · optimal 1:33.4" when the
optimal is known from the sector part, else best only); **flag flash** (a new flag: the strip takes the flag colour
for 2 s, then returns; the SSG box and the glow do the rest: `flaglow.js` and the shared flag table stay); **no data
yet** (every zone shows "—" in its place, never blank); **feed frozen** (no frame for 3 s: the screen stays, greyed,
with "no data for 12 s" in the strip).
**Refresh:** one `/stream?set=race&state=1` subscription through `lib/data.js` (15 Hz; `data.telemetry(...)` or its
`set` equivalent: extend data.js minimally and say so); interval and gap numbers update at most 4 times a second and
only when the tenth changes (F1's no-flicker rule); no other request while driving (≤ 1/s besides the stream).
**Never shows:** tyres, damage, oil/water/voltage/humidity, a second standings list, radar, input traces, a track map,
corner names. **Deck:** untouched.
**The switch:** HOME shows this page full-screen the moment `data.rig.driving` is true (a FRESH frame with IsOnTrack,
not a replay; core.js ~466 has the rule) and goes back to HOME when it has been false for 5 s; `drive.js`, `ui.js`'s
in-car grid, `drive.css` and the old `race.js` are deleted (W0-4 left them; the garage view they served is gone with
them: say in the report what else referenced them and what you did). The test-only `?drive=1` becomes `?race=1`.
