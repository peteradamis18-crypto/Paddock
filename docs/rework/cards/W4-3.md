## The screen (1920×1080 under the shell's header; nothing under 24 px; type-scale tokens; one gold fill)
- **Top line:** track · car · session type · date; for a race the result: "P4 from P7 · +41 iRating (estimate)" when
  `result.irating` is known, else "P4 of 19".
- **Left half:** `Map.mount` coloured by `map.gain`, the three corners marked 1, 2, 3 (`map.marks`).
- **Right half: the cards** (`cards[]`, at most 3, ranked): four lines each (corner label/name; `happened`; `lost` big and
  gold, "0.31 s"; "Try this: …" from `tryThis`) and the sample line ("in 6 of 8 clean laps"); under 3 clean laps
  (`note` set) one card with numbers and no advice and the note shown; a race (`race` not null) shows the **race story**
  instead: position by lap (a small line chart drawn with `Chart`'s step row or plain SVG: no library), pace against
  the field ("your average 1:34.1 · the field 1:34.8 · 19 cars"), the three costliest corners each with its clip (WATCH
  plays it through `/api/rec/session-video`'s clip route as coach.html does today), and at most one corner card marked
  "clean-air lap" (`race.cleanAir`).
- **Bottom:** the one plan line (`plan`) and two buttons: **SHOW ME** (`ctx.go('review', { screen: 'laps', corner: n })`:
  screen 2 at that corner, W4-4) and **WATCH** (the clip of card 1; greyed with "no clip" when null). A line
  "Compared with: lap 9 of this session (1:33.832)" or "your best here, 6 Nov · track 31°, 42 L fuel" from `ref`.
- **States** (fixture + picture each): practice with 8 clean laps; practice with 2 laps (one card, no advice); a race (the
  story); no complete lap ("No full lap yet" + what a partial lap gives: the note only); offline (the shell's banner;
  the page shows its last data greyed or the empty state); working ("Reading your laps…" with no spinner games: one
  line, the page re-asks on `data.onList('review')`); after a drive this page is what HOME's REVIEW card opens
  (`ctx.opts.at` = the session; `id=last` otherwise).
- The deck's REVIEW key and SHOW ME: `ctx.opts.screen` chooses 'card' (default) | 'laps' | 'more'; this page owns the
  'card' screen and hands the other two to W4-4/W4-5's modules (`app/pages/review-laps.js`, `review-more.js`: import by
  name; until they exist show "Coming with the next update").
- Request budget: one `data.json('/api/review?id=…')`, the map's one fetch, the clip on press; nothing on a timer.
