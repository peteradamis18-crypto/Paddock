# HOME redesign brief (lead, 2026-10-11) - award-level

Peter: "background pic of the C8 looks weird with the split and text overlay on the pics ... layout and design needs to be
exceptional. This will be published online for an award."

## What is wrong now (tests\shots\home.png)
- A hard vertical split: a dark panel ends at x=740 and the photo starts behind it - reads as a mistake.
- Text and boxes sit on top of the car (career numbers, LAST SESSION card, RIG ON button over the bodywork).
- Too many small blocks competing; no single focal point; the photo is darkened into mud.

## Direction
- **One composition, not a dashboard.** The car photo is the hero: shown large, uninterrupted, well lit (no heavy
  darkening), in its own space on the right ~60 % of the screen, bleeding off the right/top edges. Its left edge dissolves
  into the black background with a long, soft gradient (no visible seam, no panel edge).
- **No text on the car, ever.** Text only on true negative space (the black left side, or dark sky/track areas you
  verify in the picture). Credit line tiny, bottom right, on black.
- **Hierarchy (three levels only):** 1) name + number + current car (one confident line block), 2) the one primary
  action (RIG ON / START / DRIVE - one gold button), 3) a quiet facts row: PB per track (max 3), career totals, last
  session with REVIEW. Everything else goes.
- **Grid:** 1920x1080, 96 px outer margins, 12 columns, 24 px gutters; every block aligned to it; generous space.
- **Type:** the existing family; three sizes (hero 72-88 px name, 28-32 px values, 14-16 px labels with tracking);
  numbers tabular; gold only for the primary action and one accent.
- **Words:** docs\COPY-RULES.md - no greeting, no sentences; labels and numbers only.
- **Status:** rig/phone states become one discreet status line at the very bottom (or icons), not cards.

## References to compare against (side by side in the review)
F1 TV / Formula 1 app driver pages, Porsche Motorsport and Chevrolet Corvette Racing web heroes, Apple TV+ sports
title screens, iRacing's own UI. Calm, cinematic, lots of black, one image, one action.

## Review gate (mandatory before it counts)
1. Picture of the new HOME at 1920x1080 on the real data + side-by-side with 2 references (described or mocked).
2. Independent critique by a second agent: "would this win a design award? what looks amateur?" - fix every point.
3. The lead's look. Then Peter sees it.
