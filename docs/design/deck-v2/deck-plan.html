Three keys carry the whole loop (GET OUT, RESTART, GET IN) and two of them broke. So the rule for every key is: one press, from any state, finishes the whole job. The key works out where you are; you never do the steps.

What each key will do

GET IN

Always puts you in the car. No sim: starts your last drive. Session screen or replay: presses Drive. In the garage: goes to the pits. Already in: nothing.

GET OUT

Still rolling? It waits for the car to stop (up to 15 s), then gets out. One press.

RESTART

AI race: gets you out, restarts the race, puts you back in. Test drive: back to the pits and in the car. One press.

NEXT SESSION

AI race: practice → qualify → race. In qualify it jumps straight to the race start. Test drive: back to the menu.

RACE NOW

New. From practice or qualify, straight to the green flag of the race.

LEAVE

Opens a small page with one big QUIT TO MENU key and BACK. Opening the page is the safety check, so QUIT itself is one press. No more "press again in 3 seconds".

Every press also shows one line on the top screen for 4 seconds: what it did, or why not ("Waiting for the car to stop…", "No race running").
