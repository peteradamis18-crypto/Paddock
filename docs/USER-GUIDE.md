# Paddock — your keys, in plain English

Paddock is the name on the top screen and on the Stream Deck. The deck has three pages: **LEAVE** (one step to the left of the main page), **MAIN**, and **MORE** (one step to the right).
The main page has four rows: **before you drive → in a session → pit and car → sound and the screens after.**
Every key is one press. No key asks you to press twice. Each key works out where you are and does the right thing from there.
Every key looks the same way: a black key with one picture and one word. The colour says the job: **gold** is the one you most likely want (DRIVE), **green** starts something, **red** ends something, **grey** is a setting.
Every press shows one short line on the top screen, so you can see it was heard and what it did. A short low beep means the key was heard but nothing could happen (for example, no session is running).
The keys that end things are not on the main page. They are on the **LEAVE** page. QUIT TO MENU is one press there. SHUT DOWN PC and SLEEP PC ask once more: press YES within 5 seconds.
Every screen that opens on the deck has its red **EXIT** in the same place: bottom right. Right after the deck changes page, it ignores presses for a moment, so a key that sends its press twice never presses the key underneath on the new page.

## Main page, row 1 — before you drive
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **HOME** | Starts anything the rig needs that is not running yet (Stream Deck, the dashboard, wheel and haptics apps, the iRacing app) and puts the iRacing app on the middle screen. The PC does this by itself at start-up, so you only need it if something did not come up. It also brings the top screen back to its HOME page. During a session it does nothing, so it cannot cover the sim. It is also the second key to unlock **KIDS**. |
| **DRIVE** | Always gets you in the car. If nothing is running, it starts your last drive (a Test Drive with the car and track chosen on the AI RACE screen). If you are on the session screen or watching a replay, it presses Drive. If you are in the car already, it does nothing. |
| **AI RACE** | Opens the AI RACE screen on the deck: the car, the track, how hard the AI is, and one big green **START** (below). |
| **OFFICIAL** | Opens the iRacing app on its Official racing page. It does nothing while a session is running. |
| **FRIENDS** | Opens the friends screen on the deck. |
| **KIDS** | Locks the keyboard and mouse so the kids cannot break anything. It works while they drive. The deck then answers only **DRIVE**, **RESET CAR**, **RESTART** and **VOL**. To unlock: press **KIDS**, then **HOME** within 4 seconds. |
| **AC** | Opens the **AC screen**. The key itself shows whether the AC is on and the room temperature. |
| **MORE** | Opens the second page of keys. |

## Main page, row 2 — in a session
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **GET OUT** | Gets you out of the car. If the car is still rolling, it waits for it to stop (up to 15 seconds), then gets out. |
| **RESET CAR** | If the car is still moving, waits until it stops, then tows you to the pits and back into the car. Instant in practice. In a race, iRacing adds its tow time. |
| **RESTART** | Restarts the AI race and puts you back in the car. In a Test Drive it takes you back to the pits instead. Official sessions cannot be restarted. |
| **TO THE RACE** | One press to the race. In an AI race weekend (practice or qualifying) it goes straight to the race session and puts you in the car; in the race itself it just gets you in. A Test Drive has no race: the key says so and the drive goes on (to finish it, use QUIT TO MENU on the LEAVE page). Official sessions cannot be skipped, and it says so. |
| **PAUSE** | Freezes an AI race or a Test Drive. Press again to carry on. The top screen says Paused or Running again. iRacing does not allow it online, and the key says so. |
| **SAVE SPOT** | Test Drive practice loop. Remembers exactly where the car is: speed, gear, fuel, tyres (iRacing's Active Reset). Press it just before the corner you want to work on. |
| **BACK TO SPOT** | Puts the car straight back at the saved spot, as many times as you like. Paddock times every try from the spot to the exit of the corner, and the top screen says it: Try 3: 9.82 s - best 9.61 (+0.21). Saving a new spot starts the count over. Works in a Test Drive only. |
| **LEAVE** | Opens the **LEAVE** page (below). |

## Main page, row 3 — pit and car (pit keys only work while you are in the car)
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **FUEL TO FINISH** | Asks iRacing for enough fuel to finish. |
| **TIRES** | Switches the four-tyre change at your next stop on or off. The key does not show which; the pit screen in iRacing does. |
| **FAST REPAIR** | Switches a fast repair at your next stop on or off. |
| **CLEAR PIT** | Clears everything from your pit stop request. |
| **BEST LAP CAR** | Shows or hides iRacing's reference car (your best lap) on the track. |
| **REPLAY** | Opens the replay screen on the deck. |
The last two keys of this row are empty on purpose.

## Main page, row 4 — sound, and the screens after a drive
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **VOL − / VOL +** | Turns the sim and the iRacing app down or up, 5 % a press. The spotter keeps its own level, so it never goes quiet with the game. It does not touch the PC's main volume. |
| **SPOTTER** | One press mutes the spotter's voice; press again to bring it back. The sim and the music keep playing. The spotter's own level is on the **SOUND** screen. |
| **SOUND** | Opens the sound screen: game, spotter, music, speakers and ButtKicker levels (the spotter's level is the second column), mute, and presets. |
| **OVERLAYS** | Opens the overlays screen on the deck. The key shows how many overlays are on. |
| **RACES** | Opens the **RACES screen**: every race Paddock filmed, newest first (below). |
| **REVIEW** | Opens the review of your last drive on the top screen (the lap analysis and the Vault are in there). |
| **RIG** | Opens the RIG page on the top screen. The dot on the key is the last rig check: green all good, amber something to look at, red something broken, grey not checked yet. |

## The LEAVE page (one step left of the main page)
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **BACK** (bottom left) | Back to the main page. |
| **QUIT TO MENU** | Leaves the whole session and takes you back to the iRacing app's home page. It is two keys wide so it is easy to hit. One press does it. |
| **SHUT DOWN PC** | Asks once: a big gold **YES, SHUT DOWN** key counts 5-4-3-2-1. Press YES within 5 seconds and the PC turns off (it leaves iRacing first, and it refuses if the car is moving). Press nothing and the question goes away by itself; EXIT (bottom right) leaves at once. Kids mode blocks it. After it, the deck starts on the main page. |
| **SLEEP PC** | Asks once, the same way: press **YES, SLEEP** within 5 seconds. It leaves iRacing first, and it refuses if the car is moving. When the PC wakes up, the deck is on the main page. |

## Second page (MORE)
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **BACK** (bottom left) | Back to the main page. |
| **THANKS** | Sends "Thanks!" in the race chat. |
| **AC COOLER** / **AC WARMER** | Sets the AC about 1 °F cooler or warmer at once, without opening the screen. If the AC is off it comes on, cooling. |
| **LOCK** | Locks the PC (Windows sign-in screen). It does not stop iRacing. |

## The AI RACE screen (the AI RACE key on the main page)
Everything for an AI race on one page. Nothing starts until you press **START**.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **RECENT**, **CARS**, **TRACKS**, **FAVOURITES** | Lists, one key per car, track or drive. RECENT starts with your last drive. Pick a car and the tracks come up; pick a track (and its layout, if it has more than one) and you are back on this page. **BACK** (top left in a list) comes back without choosing. Nothing to type. |
| the car, the track, **LAYOUT** | What is chosen now. |
| **DAMAGE** | Damage on or off for the drive. |
| **AI LEVEL**: **LOW**, **MEDIUM**, **HIGH** | How hard the AI pushes you in the next AI race (iRacing's Adaptive AI: it follows your pace and keeps the race close). The gold one is what the race will use. |
| **RANGE** | Instead of Adaptive AI, a fixed skill range: each press steps 25-50, 50-75, 75-100, then back to off (Adaptive). The AI LEVEL key says which one the race will use. |
| **START** (big, green) | Starts the AI race with that car, track and level, and puts you in the car. If the iRacing app is closed, it opens it first. The deck goes back to the main page a moment later, where TO THE RACE and GET OUT are. In kids mode the screen only says KIDS MODE IS ON. |
| **EXIT** | Back to your keys. |
The same car and track are what **DRIVE** starts when nothing is running.

## The RACES screen (the RACES key on the main page)
Paddock films every session you drive, start to finish, with the game and spotter sound. The RACES screen lists them, newest first; gold ones are kept for good.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| a race | Opens it: track, day and time, length and laps, best lap, size on disk. |
| **SHARE ON YOUTUBE** | Opens YouTube's upload page and shows the race's file selected in **Videos\Paddock**: drag the file into YouTube. |
| **DELETE FROM PC** | Press twice: the race goes to the trash. It is deleted for good after 14 days. |
| **WATCH** | Plays it on the middle screen. |
| **KEEP** | Only on a practice or qualifying video: keeps it for good. Races are kept by themselves. |
| **BACK / EXIT** | Back to the list / back to your keys. |

**On the top screen:** press **RACES** at the top. The RACES page shows every race as a card with a picture from the race. Each card says when it was, the track, the car, how long, how many laps, your best lap ("no clean lap" when iRacing counted none) and its size. One line says where it is with YouTube: **Not shared**, **Waiting to share**, **Sharing 41%**, **On YouTube (private)**, **On YouTube**, **No internet, waiting**, **YouTube limit: trying again after 9** (YouTube's daily upload limit; it goes by itself the next morning), or the reason it could not go. **Kept** means it stays until you delete it.
- **Tabs:** **RACES** (races only), **PRACTICE & QUALIFYING**, **ALL**.
- **Tick a card** (the box top left) and a bar appears at the bottom: **SHARE TO YOUTUBE**, **DELETE**, **CANCEL**. Hold SHIFT and tick another card to tick everything in between. Switching tabs clears the ticks. **SELECT ALL SHARED** ticks every race that is already on YouTube (handy for freeing space).
- **DELETE** works at once, with no question. The races go to the trash and a note says "Deleted 3 races · UNDO" for 8 seconds: press **UNDO** to bring them back. The trash empties itself after 14 days.
- **SHARE TO YOUTUBE**: Paddock cuts off the out lap and the time after the finish, names the video (see YouTube titles below), adds chapters (start, incidents, pit stops, best lap, finish) and uploads it. All of this waits until you are out of the car, so it never slows a session. A race goes to YouTube only once.
- **OPEN** shows one race big: **WATCH** (plays it on the middle screen, from the start), **SHARE TO YOUTUBE**, **TALK OVER IT** (see below), **REVIEW** (what to change next time, for that race) and **DELETE**. Under them the moments of the race in time order. When it is on YouTube, its link is there with **COPY LINK**. **BACK TO ALL RACES** or Esc goes back.
- **The line at the top** says how much the races use, how much is free and about how many more races fit. When the trash holds 1 GB or more it says so, with **EMPTY NOW**. When the disk is nearly full, one gold key appears: **Free up space: delete the 6 oldest shared races (7 GB)**. It only ever deletes races that are already on YouTube, never a race you kept.
- **YouTube, once:** if Paddock is not signed in to YouTube yet, a line with **SIGN IN TO YOUTUBE** appears as soon as you tick or open a race. Press it once and sign in to Google, then press SHARE. Until then SHARE says "Sign in to YouTube first" and nothing is cut.
- **Top right:** "Uploads are private · **MAKE PUBLIC**" (one press switches new uploads between private and public) and **YOUTUBE TITLES**: the ways a video can be named, shown with your latest race. Press the one you like.
- While you drive the page only says "Back after your drive". If the dashboard stops answering, one line says so and the rest stays as it was.

**Talk over a race.** Put the headset on, open the race (on the deck or the top screen) and press **TALK OVER IT**. The race plays on
the middle screen with the game sound, and Paddock records what you say from the same moment. The deck shows **TALKING 03:12**, your
microphone's name and one big red **STOP**; the top screen shows the same. Talk as long as you like and press **STOP** (or let the race
play to the end). Paddock then mixes it by itself in a few seconds: the game goes quieter while you talk. The race then says
**With commentary: ready** with **SHARE WITH COMMENTARY** (on the deck: **SHARE COMMENTARY**). One press puts it on YouTube as a second video
named "... (with commentary)"; the first video stays as it is.
- "No microphone found. Plug in the headset and press again." means Windows sees no microphone: plug it in and press TALK OVER IT again.
- "The microphone is silent. Is it muted?" (amber) means it hears nothing for 5 seconds: check the mute button. The recording goes on.
- If a session starts while you talk, Paddock stops at once and keeps what you said. **EXIT** on the deck does not stop a talk; STOP does.

**Without the deck:** every race is also a normal file in **Videos\Paddock**, named by date, track and car. On YouTube press **Select files**, open **Videos** then **Paddock**, and pick it.

## The AC screen (the AC key on the main page)
Everything for the room AC on one page. A press shows on the keys at once; the AC follows a few seconds later, because the command goes through Midea's server the same way the Midea Air app does. While it is on its way, the ROOM key says SENDING. Several quick presses go out together.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AC ON / AC OFF** | Turns the AC on or off, like the remote's power button. Turning it off pauses auto-cool for an hour (the remote or the app counts too). |
| **ROOM** | The room temperature from the AC's own thermometer, and what the AC is doing. |
| **− COOLER / TARGET / + WARMER** | The temperature the AC aims for, about 1 °F per press. |
| **MAX COOL / COMFORT / QUIET** | One press sets everything: 64 °F fan high / 72 °F fan auto / 72 °F fan low. |
| **COOL, AUTO, DRY, FAN ONLY, HEAT** | The mode. The lit one is what the AC is doing. |
| **LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, AUTO** | The fan. In Auto or Dry mode the AC locks its fan, so picking a speed switches it to Cool. |
| **AUTO-COOL ON / OFF** | Lets Paddock cool the room while the PC is on (below). |
| **− START / STARTS AT / + START** | The room temperature at which auto-cool starts. It stops 4 °F lower. |
| **EXIT** | Back to your keys. |
**Auto-cool.** While the PC is on, Paddock checks the room every 2 minutes using the AC's own thermometer. At 76 °F or warmer (change it on the AC screen) it turns the AC on, cooling to 70 °F. At 72 °F or cooler it turns it off again, but only if auto-cool turned it on. SLEEP PC and SHUT DOWN PC also turn off cooling that auto-cool started. The top screen says what the AC is doing whenever a key is pressed or auto-cool switches it. Each AC key takes about 7 seconds, because the command goes through Midea's server, the same way the Midea Air app does it.


## Screens on the deck

**Friends.** A friend uses the deck: their name, a car, a track, and a wheel strength (Light, Medium or Full). START puts them in the car with damage off. Light and Medium are easier than your wheel. Full is your normal strength. The top screen names that feel. It does not show a live strength number, because the sim's number stays on your own wheel. EXIT FRIENDS puts your wheel back, even if the session closed first. While they drive, the top screen shows the track, their car as a gold dot, the current lap counting, the last lap, the best lap and the session clock. A best lap shows in purple. Being ahead of a best shows in green. COACH turns the track map on or off. It starts on. START sends the racing-line key when the line is set to Shown.

**Overlays.** Switch each overlay on or off. Choose a preset: RACE, PRACTICE, QUALI, MINIMAL or FRIENDS. Move them with EDIT LAYOUT. The delta bar is on this screen too: on or off, and which lap it compares (below).



**Replay.** Press REPLAY; EXIT (bottom right) returns to your keys. You can play and pause, rewind and fast forward, slow motion, jump to a lap or an incident, go back 10 seconds, and watch your car, the car ahead, the car behind, the leader, or any car in the list. Out of a session the screen lists your saved replays. While you are driving, the keys are dim: get out of the car before you watch a replay. EXIT still works. Changing the camera group, hiding the iRacing menus, and My last incident are still being finished. PHOTO (top left) takes a photo without the menus; it is saved for the side screens. If the picture is too small, the key says so. If screen capture is off, the key tells you to switch it on in iRacing's settings, and it does not take the photo.

## The top screen

Four doors along the top: **HOME · RACES · REVIEW · RIG**, the same four words as on the deck. The one you are on is gold. Next to PADDOCK is today's date. One page shows at a time.

- **HOME** is the start. (It is being rebuilt: for now it says so.)
- **RACES** is every race you recorded (above).
- **REVIEW** shows what to change next time, for your last drive (below: REVIEW: the card). Pressing REVIEW on a race in RACES opens that race here.
- **RIG** shows how the PC ran (Performance, below).

To get back to HOME from any page: press **CLOSE** (top right), or **Esc** on the keyboard, or the HOME key on the deck. (Inside a page, Esc first closes what that page has open, like a lap or a video.) A key on the deck that opens a page (RACES, REVIEW, the old VAULT and PERFORMANCE keys) opens it here.

One line at the bottom of the screen tells you the one thing you need to know, never two at once, in this order: **Paddock is not running. Press HOME on the deck.** (the screen cannot reach Paddock; the doors still work), kids mode, **Reset car · waiting for the car to stop**, and what the last deck key did (for 4 seconds).

While you drive: the race screen (below) covers everything, and no page stays open behind it. A coloured edge when a flag is out. Five seconds after you stop, HOME is back.
### The top screen: HOME

When the rig is idle: **HOME**. It is quiet on purpose, like a good watch.

- **In the middle: the clock**, your own local time. When one of your races starts within a day, it says **NEXT GREEN FLAG** and how long to go. In the last 30 minutes a thin gold ring closes around the clock.
- **On the left: your next races.** The series you race (the ones you made favourites, and the ones Paddock recognises from your last 10 official races: same car, same track, same week; it remembers them when a new season starts), with the time it starts, the track and your car. The next one is big; a race that has already started fades. If none of your series is racing soon, it says **Next race you can join** and shows the next race you have the car and track for. When a session is loaded it says **NOW**: the session, the track, your car and when it was loaded (and the series, when one of your races starts there within the hour). The countdown stops while a session is loaded.
- **On the right: MY RACES.** Your last three drives: the track, the day, and where you finished (for example **P4 of 19**; in a race with more than one class, your place in your class). **LAST RACE** opens the review. For ten minutes after a drive shows up there, a card says how it went (**Your race: P3 from P9**) with a **REVIEW** button.
- **At the bottom: the rig.** **Rig ready** in green with what it checked (iRacing, deck, spotter, overlays), or one sentence saying what is wrong, with a **FIX IT** button when the Rig Doctor can fix it itself (it then says **Checking the rig**).
- **One gold button** that fits the moment: **RIG ON** when the rig is off; **DRIVE** when a session is loaded (it gets you in the car), or when nothing is running (it starts your last car at your last track, named under it); **START** if there is no last drive yet. While it works it says what it is doing; if it cannot, it says why on the button. In kids mode or when Paddock is not running it turns grey and says why.

Home never shows while you drive. It no longer shows the real-world TV schedule, the phone code (it moves to RIG) or statistics.

### REVIEW: the card

After every drive, **REVIEW** (the door, the deck key, or REVIEW on HOME's card) shows one screen: what to change next time.

- **At the top:** the track, your car, the kind of session and the day. After a race, how it went: **P4 from P7 · +41 iRating (estimate)** (or **P4 of 19** when the iRating change is not known).
- **On the left: the track map.** Red where you lost time, green where you gained, against the lap named at the bottom. The numbers 1, 2, 3 on it are the cards on the right.
- **On the right: up to three cards**, the corner where you lost the most time first. Each card: the corner (**Turn 5**), what happened in plain words (**You braked 14 m earlier than your best lap**), the time it cost, big and gold (**0.31 s**), how often (**in 6 of 8 clean laps**), and one thing to try (**Try this: Brake at the 100 m board**). Press a card to see that corner on the laps screen.
- With fewer than 3 clean laps there is one card with the numbers and no advice: too few laps to be sure. It says so.
- **After a race** the right side tells the race instead: your place lap by lap, your average lap against the field's (**Your average 1:34.1 · the field 1:34.8 · 19 cars**), the three corners that cost you most, each with **WATCH**, and at most one card from a lap in clean air.
- **At the bottom:** the plan for next time in one line (**Next time: Turn 5, brake at the 100 m board, check the entry speed**), which lap everything is compared with (**Compared with: lap 9 of this session (1:33.832)**, or your best here with the day, track temperature and fuel when this drive had too few clean laps), and two buttons: **SHOW ME** (the laps screen, at the first card's corner) and **WATCH** (your own video of that corner, 10 seconds; **no clip** when this drive was not recorded). Esc or the video's own **CLOSE** puts it away (if the video cannot be found it says **No video of this corner**).
- **No full lap yet:** it says so, with **MORE DATA** for what the drive did record.
- While Paddock reads your laps (a minute or so after a long session) it says **Reading your laps…** and fills in by itself. When Paddock is not running, the last review shows greyed out.

**Sessions & laps.** Every session you have driven: date, car, track, how long, best lap. Filter by car or track. Tap a session to see the laps, then one lap against another: speed, throttle, brake, and where time went. Your best at each car and track is listed, with the same comparison against the field and real IMSA times.

**Performance.** The last session's frame rate, the slowest places on the track, what else was using the PC, whether a change you made helped, and a short checklist of what is already right. Nothing on this page changes the graphics.

### REVIEW: the laps

One lap against another, the way the pros look at it. **SHOW ME** on a review card opens it at that corner.

- **Top left: the track map.** A dot shows where you are looking. Green is where this lap was quicker than the reference, red where it was slower.
- **Two rows of laps.** **THIS LAP** is the lap you look at, **REFERENCE** the lap you compare it with. Press a lap to change it. Laps that count are bright; the others are dim and say why (out lap, in lap, off track). Your best lap's time is purple. With fewer than 3 clean laps there is also **Your best here**: your best ever with this car at this track.
- **Under the laps:** this lap against the reference ("Lap 8 1:34.020 against lap 9 1:33.832 · +0.188 s"), and your **theoretical best**: your best bits joined into one lap, with the laps and days they came from.
- **The traces**, one above the other on the same stretch of track: the **delta** in gold (going up = losing time: that is where to look), speed, throttle and brake, steering, gear. The column on the right always shows both laps' numbers at the line, big. Scroll the mouse wheel on the traces to zoom in.
- **The corner table** under the traces: for every corner, this lap, the reference and the difference (green = quicker, red = slower) for the brake point, the minimum speed, where you were back on the throttle, and the time through the corner. What each one means is written once underneath. Press a corner to zoom to it.
- **The deck's REVIEW arrows** go to the next or previous corner, zoom in, and back to the whole lap.

If no lap is finished yet it says **No full lap yet**. While Paddock reads a new session it says **Reading your laps…**. Nothing needs typing.

## The race screen

The top screen changes by itself the moment you drive out. Five or so seconds after you stop (a tow, a reset, the end of the session, a replay, or the sim or Paddock not answering), HOME comes back (or the page you asked for on the deck while you were driving). You press nothing. In Friends mode your guest keeps their own screen: the setup card, then their lap timer and leaderboard while they drive.

- **Top strip.** Your position (and your class position in a multiclass race), the lap and how many to go (or, in a race run on time, the time left), your incidents against the limit (the box turns amber when you are 3 or fewer from it), and the race clock. When a new flag comes out, the strip turns that flag's colour and says its name for 2 seconds. The light box and the coloured edge keep showing it after that. Under a caution the strip says **CAUTION** in amber for the whole caution.
- **Gaps.** The car ahead and the car behind: the gap in seconds, their number and name. Green means it is going your way (closing on the car ahead, pulling away from the car behind), red means the other way. The small arrow is how much per lap, over the last 3 laps. Under a caution this becomes your gap to the leader.
- **Fuel.** The big number is laps of fuel left against laps to go: **+1.7** (green) means you will finish with that much spare, **−0.6** (red) means you are short. A little is always kept back (it says how much). In a race run on time, laps to go is an estimate and has a ~ in front. Before 3 clean laps it says it is still measuring; after a stop the old numbers go grey until it has measured again. Fuel is in litres, or in gallons if your iRacing is set to imperial. When a stop is needed, a row of small blocks shows each lap left: green is the pit window, orange is too early, red is too late, white means no stop on that lap gets you to the end. When you are within a lap of making it, it tells you how much to save per lap. Under a caution it asks **PIT NOW?** with the fuel you need and the fuel you have.
- **Your lap.** Your last lap (purple when it is your best), your best, and how far you are ahead (green) or behind (red) of your best right now, with a bar.
- **Standings.** Your class only: the leader on top, then the 4 cars ahead of you, you in gold, and the 4 behind. Each car shows its class position, number, name and the gap to the car in front. Cars in the pits are grey with a **PIT** tag. Under a caution the gaps are to the leader.

In the pits the gaps and your lap fold away; the fuel side shows what is in the tank and **LIMITER ON** while your pit limiter is on. Once Paddock has measured how long a stop takes at this track, a gold row in the standings shows where you will come back out (**YOU REJOIN P7**).

In practice and qualifying the standings become this session's laps (newest first, your best in purple, each with its gap to your best), the gaps become your best lap, and the strip shows the time left.

If the data stops, the screen stays, greyed, and the strip says how long it has had nothing. Numbers change at most four times a second, so nothing flickers. There are no tyres, no damage, no map and no radar on this screen. The small frame-rate number and the amber frame-rate line of the old screen are gone on purpose: the black box and the RIG page tell you the frame rate after the drive.

## The delta bar

A slim bar on the middle screen while you drive. Green means ahead, red means behind. It compares with the best lap you have ever done in this car at this track. That is the usual setting (VS PB). Until you have one, it uses this session's best lap.

On the overlays screen, VS steps through four comparisons: your best ever, your best lap this session, the best your sectors could add up to, and the fastest lap anyone set this session. The bar hides in the pits. It never takes over the screen.

## The side screens

When iRacing is not running, both side screens show the same photo. The photo changes every two minutes, with a slow fade. One small line of text in the corner. No logo. The moment the sim starts, the photos go away. They come back when the sim has closed. Photos from the replay screen join this set.

## REVIEW: more data

On the REVIEW screen, **MORE DATA** opens every number of one lap, in four tabs along the top. (The deck's REVIEW key and **SHOW ME** lead to it; press **Esc** or **CLOSE** to go back. Esc first undoes a zoom, then goes HOME.)

- **DRIVER**: the track map coloured by where you gain or lose, and speed, throttle, brake and steering over the lap, against the reference lap. **+ CHANNEL** adds any other channel the car records (tyres, suspension, grip, ride height and the rest) as one more line on the same picture.
- **BRAKING**: where you brake, how hard, how long you hold it, and any lock-ups, corner by corner.
- **SPLITS**: your sector times, the best lap your sectors could add up to, and how steady you are.
- **RACE**: only for a race Paddock logged. The final order, how the gap to the leader changed lap by lap, positions, your passes, and you against the car ahead and the leader, corner by corner.

Three buttons at the bottom of the screen:

- **EXPORT CSV** saves the lap and its reference, every metre, to your Downloads folder.
- **OPEN IN PI TOOLBOX** opens the whole file in Cosworth's standard layout. It only appears when Pi Toolbox is installed.
- **OPEN DELTA** starts Coach Dave Delta. It only appears when Delta is installed, and it never opens while you drive.

The old GRIP, TYRES, SUSPENSION, VIDEO, CAREER and PRO pages are gone. Your progress over many sessions is in the progress part of REVIEW. Your race videos are in RACES.

## The video recorder

Paddock films the middle race screen while you are in the car. It is on. It keeps the last three minutes. When a lap is your best, the clip of that lap is kept in the Vault. Other laps are not kept after the last three minutes have passed. It never films while you are out of the car. It costs about 1.5 frames per second. Clips play from RACES and on your iPhone under **LAPS › VIDEOS**. If you ever want it off, say so.

## Your iPhone

Paddock runs on your iPhone over the home Wi-Fi, as an app on the Home Screen. Nothing to install from a store.

1. **Once:** double-click `tools\phone-allow.cmd` on the PC and click Yes. It lets the phone in (your own network only) and nothing else. `tools\phone-undo.cmd` takes it back.
2. Within half a minute the top screen's Home shows a small square. Point the iPhone **Camera** at it and tap the link (or open the address it shows and type the six-digit code under it).
3. In Safari: **Share › Add to Home Screen**. From then on, tap **Paddock** on the phone.

If Home says **One-time Windows permission needed**, step 1 has not been done yet. The code is good for ten minutes and pairs up to three phones.

The phone has the same keys as the deck, nothing more: **HOME** (what the rig is doing and the one big button), **DRIVE** (**KEYS**: every deck key, grouped like the deck; **REPLAY**; **OVERLAYS**), **SOUND**, **SETUP** (the deck's My Drive: pick a car and track, search, **START** is always at the bottom), **LAPS** (**SESSIONS**: every lap, where time went, the race and the career numbers; **VIDEOS**: your lap videos, watch, **Save**, **Share**, and **Make share video**, see below). A bar at the top always shows rig, sim and car; the page's main button sits above the tab bar. Pressing the tab you are on, or swiping back, closes an opened session or video. While you are in the car it only offers what the deck offers then. A phone you no longer use: Home › **Paired phones › Forget**.

## Share a lap, and see it in iRacing

**Share video.** Paddock films your best laps by itself. To send one to a friend or put it on YouTube, open the lap on your iPhone (**LAPS › VIDEOS**, tap the lap) and press **Make share video**. The PC adds the numbers on top (speed, gear, throttle and brake, the lap time running, how far you are ahead or behind your best other lap, a small map with the car on it, your name and number, and a small Paddock mark), in 1080p, the right way up. It takes about a minute and only starts when you are out of the car. When it says **Ready to share**, press **Save** (then find it in Files and share it from there) or **Share** (only where the phone offers its share sheet). On the PC, the same button is in the analysis, on the **VIDEO** page: **MAKE SHARE VIDEO**, then **SHOW SHARE VIDEO** opens the folder with the file selected.

**See it in iRacing.** In the analysis, every event on a lap (ABS, lock-up, wheel slip) and every pass in a race has a small **SEE IN iRACING** button. It takes iRacing's own replay to that moment, three seconds before, on your car (or the car that passed you), playing. The button only shows while iRacing has that same session open and you are out of the car; otherwise it stays hidden.

## What happens by itself

- The PC starts the rig when you log on. Stream Deck comes up first, then the dashboard, the wheel, the haptics, the overlays, the spotter, and the iRacing app.
- iRacing is given the PC first when a session starts.
- Rig Doctor checks the rig at startup and now and then while you are out of the car. It fixes what it can by itself: overlays, the spotter, Stream Deck, and keeping iRacing's files on this PC. The idle screen says "All good" when there is nothing for you to do. It also shows a line for the last session's frame rate. It only asks you about things only you can do, such as a cable.
- Nothing restarts, pops up, or reloads the dashboard while you are driving. A new version of the top screen waits until you are out of the car.

**Sharing to YouTube straight from Paddock:** do the one-time Google setup in `docs\YOUTUBE-SETUP.md` (about 10 minutes, once).
