# Paddock — your keys, in plain English

Paddock is the name on the top screen and on the Stream Deck. The main page has four rows, one per stage of a session:
**get into a car → in a session → in the car → talk, sound, PC.**
Keys that END something sit on the right. Keys marked **x2** need a second press within 3 seconds. The first press beeps twice, so a bump cannot fire them.

## Row 1 — get into a car
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **RIG ON** | Starts everything the rig needs, in order: Stream Deck, the dashboard, wheel and haptics apps, then the iRacing app on the middle screen. Runs by itself when the PC starts, so you only need it if something did not come up. |
| **AI RACE · LAST SETTINGS** | One press: an AI race with the same settings as last time. It loads and puts you in the car. |
| **TEST DRIVE · LAST SETTINGS** | One press: a practice session in the same car at the same track as last time. It loads and puts you in the car. |
| **FRIENDS MODE** | Opens the friends screen on the deck. |
| **OFFICIAL RACING** | Opens the iRacing app on its Official racing page. |
| **SHOWCASE · INTRO** | Replays the gold startup animation on the top screen. It is only for show and does not change anything. |
| **SKIP SESSION** | Moves to the next session (for example practice, then qualifying, then the race), including iRacing's second "are you sure". |
| **DRIVE · CONTINUE** | Presses whatever iRacing's next green button is: Drive, Race, or the launch button. Use it to get back into the car. |

## Row 2 — in a session
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **GET OUT** | Gets out of the car. The car must be stopped. |
| **TOW TO PITS** | If the car is still moving, waits until it stops (about 20 seconds), then out and back in at your pit box. Instant in practice. In a race, iRacing adds its tow time. |
| **SAVE · RESET POINT** | In Test Drive: remembers where you are on track. |
| **BACK TO · RESET POINT** | In Test Drive: puts you back at the saved spot, so you can practise one corner again. |
| **OVERLAYS SCREEN** | Opens the overlays screen on the deck. |
| **REPLAY** | Opens the replay screen on the deck. |
| **RESTART RACE x2** | Restarts the current session, including iRacing's own confirmation. |
| **QUIT TO MAIN** | Leaves the whole session and takes you back to the iRacing app's home page. |

## Row 3 — in the car (pit keys only work while you are in the car)
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **FUEL TO FINISH** | Tells iRacing to put in just enough fuel to finish at your next stop. |
| **TIRES ON / OFF** | Switches the four-tyre change at your next stop on or off. |
| **FAST REPAIR** | Switches using a fast repair at your next stop on or off. |
| **TEAR OFF** | Switches a windscreen tear-off at your next stop on or off. |
| **CLEAR PIT** | Clears everything from your pit stop request. |
| **SEAT LOWER / HIGHER** | Moves your view down or up. |
| **LAPS TO EMPTY** | Live: how many laps your fuel lasts. |

## Row 4 — talk, sound, PC
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **SORRY / THANKS** | Sends "Sorry!" or "Thanks!" in the race chat. |
| **VOL − / VOL + / MUTE** | PC volume. |
| **POSITION · CLASS** | Live: your position in your class, and how many cars. |
| **INCIDENTS** | Live: your incident points this session. |
| **SLEEP x2** | Puts the PC to sleep. |

## Screens on the deck

**Friends.** A friend uses the deck: their name, a car, a track, and a wheel strength (Light, Medium or Full). START puts them in the car with damage off. Light and Medium are easier than your wheel. Full is your normal strength. The top screen names that feel. It does not show a live strength number, because the sim's number stays on your own wheel. EXIT FRIENDS puts your wheel back, even if the session closed first. While they drive, the top screen shows the track, their car as a gold dot, the current lap counting, the last lap, the best lap and the session clock. A best lap shows in purple. Being ahead of a best shows in green. COACH turns the track map on or off. It starts on. START sends the racing-line key when the line is set to Shown.

**Overlays.** Switch each overlay on or off. Choose a preset: RACE, PRACTICE, QUALI, MINIMAL or FRIENDS. Move them with EDIT LAYOUT. The delta bar is on this screen too: on or off, and which lap it compares (below).

**My Drive.** Choose any car and track you own, then START. Recent drives and favourites are at the top. Search by typing on the deck. Damage on or off. It sets up the Test Drive, launches, and puts you in the car. It is not on the main page yet.

**Replay.** Press REPLAY, then BACK (top left) to return to your keys. You can play and pause, rewind and fast forward, slow motion, jump to a lap or an incident, go back 10 seconds, and watch your car, the car ahead, the car behind, the leader, or any car in the list. Out of a session the screen lists your saved replays. While you are driving, the keys are dim: get out of the car before you watch a replay. BACK still works. Changing the camera group, hiding the iRacing menus, and My last incident are still being finished. A photo you take here is saved for the side screens. If the picture is too small, the key says so. If screen capture is off, the key tells you to switch it on in iRacing's settings, and it does not take the photo.

## The top screen

Before you get in the car, a short brief: the track, your best lap there, and how much fuel a lap usually takes. It fades when you get in.

While you drive: the live dashboard. A coloured edge when a flag is out. A small frame-rate number next to the track name. One quiet amber line only when the frame rate is clearly under your usual for this car and track, with the likely reason. In a race it also shows the strength of the field and a rough iRating change. The turns on the map are numbered from the start line. A turn with a real name shows that name.

Afterwards: the debrief. When you set a personal best, a small comparison sits with it: your lap against other iRacing drivers (once enough of them have driven it) and against real IMSA times.

When the rig is idle: **Home**. Your name and your number, with the name of the car you drove last (the number is that car's number), your personal best at each track (iRacing's own times only), your career numbers and your week, your last session with a **REVIEW** button (the Session Review opens over Home; **‹ HOME** at the top brings it back), and one big gold button that fits the moment: **RIG ON** when the rig is not up, **LAST DRIVE** when it is, **GET IN** when a session is open. Top right: the code to scan with your iPhone (below). Quiet links at the top: **Sessions & laps**, **Vault**, **Performance**. Home never shows while you drive.

Home only counts real drives. A test, or a Test Drive where the car sat in the pit box and never went out, is not an outing: it is not in your last outing, your seat time, THIS WEEK, or the car shown. The small line **Since** and a month and year (when your first session was) is only a date, not a number of anything.

The Corvette picture: until your own shot exists, Home shows a credited photo of a real Corvette Z06 GT3.R. Drop your own as `paddock\home\hero.jpg` and Home uses it at once.

**Sessions & laps.** Every session you have driven: date, car, track, how long, best lap. Filter by car or track. Tap a session to see the laps, then one lap against another: speed, throttle, brake, and where time went. Your best at each car and track is listed, with the same comparison against the field and real IMSA times.

**Performance.** The last session's frame rate, the slowest places on the track, what else was using the PC, whether a change you made helped, and a short checklist of what is already right. Nothing on this page changes the graphics.

## The delta bar

A slim bar on the middle screen while you drive. Green means ahead, red means behind. It compares with the best lap you have ever done in this car at this track. That is the usual setting (VS PB). Until you have one, it uses this session's best lap.

On the overlays screen, VS steps through four comparisons: your best ever, your best lap this session, the best your sectors could add up to, and the fastest lap anyone set this session. The bar hides in the pits. It never takes over the screen.

## The side screens

When iRacing is not running, both side screens show the same photo. The photo changes every two minutes, with a slow fade. One small line of text in the corner. No logo. The moment the sim starts, the photos go away. They come back when the sim has closed. Photos from the replay screen join this set.

## The analysis pages

Open a lap from **Sessions & laps**, the Vault or the session review (press **ANALYSIS**). The tabs along the top are the pages. On the deck, the **ANALYSIS** screen shows the same pages as keys along the bottom row. With more than eight pages, the last key is **PAGES**: it opens a screen with every page as a big key, and **BACK** returns to the analysis keys. Pick a page there and it shows at once. A page only appears when it has something to show.

- **DRIVER**: the track map coloured by where you gain or lose, and speed, throttle, brake and steering over the lap, against your best lap.
- **GRIP**: how hard the car is working: the grip circle, slip, which end lets go.
- **BRAKES**: where you brake, how hard, how long you hold it, and any lock-ups, corner by corner.
- **TYRES**: temperatures, pressures and wear over the stint, and fuel per lap.
- **SUSP.**: ride height and the dampers.
- **SPLITS**: your sector times, the best lap your sectors could add up to, and how steady you are.
- **VIDEO**: the clip of that lap, moving in step with the traces (see the video recorder below).
- **RACE**: appears for a race Paddock logged. The final order, how the gap to the leader changed lap by lap, positions, your passes, and you against the car ahead and the leader, corner by corner.
- **CAREER**: appears once you have two or more sessions in the same car at the same track. Your best lap of every session, how each corner has got better or worse since you started, your best possible lap against your best lap, and your races there. It only counts laps iRacing counted.
- **PRO**: the tools Cosworth's Pi charges for. Pick any two channels and plot one against the other, with a fitted curve and a colour (for example lateral g against speed). A histogram of any channel (how much of the lap it spends at each value). Your own maths channels: type a formula such as `if(Speed > 50, 100 / Speed, 0)`; Paddock explains in plain words if it cannot read it, and saves your formulas for that car. EXPORT: one gold **EXCEL** button (or **CSV**) saves the lap's channels to your Downloads folder.

## The video recorder

Paddock films the middle race screen while you are in the car. It is on. It keeps the last three minutes. When a lap is your best, the clip of that lap is kept in the Vault. Press **KEEP** on any other lap you want to hold on to. It never films while you are out of the car. It costs about 1.5 frames per second. Clips play on the **VIDEO** page and on your iPhone under **LAPS › VIDEOS**. If you ever want it off, say so.

## Your iPhone

Paddock runs on your iPhone over the home Wi-Fi, as an app on the Home Screen. Nothing to install from a store.

1. **Once:** double-click `tools\phone-allow.cmd` on the PC and click Yes. It lets the phone in (your own network only) and nothing else. `tools\phone-undo.cmd` takes it back.
2. Within half a minute the top screen's Home shows a small square. Point the iPhone **Camera** at it and tap the link (or open the address it shows and type the six-digit code under it).
3. In Safari: **Share › Add to Home Screen**. From then on, tap **Paddock** on the phone.

If Home says **One-time Windows permission needed**, step 1 has not been done yet. The code is good for ten minutes and pairs up to three phones.

The phone has the same keys as the deck, nothing more: **HOME** (what the rig is doing and the one big button), **DRIVE** (**KEYS**: every deck key, grouped like the deck, the ones marked ×2 work like the deck's x2; **REPLAY**; **OVERLAYS**), **SOUND**, **SETUP** (the deck's My Drive: pick a car and track, search, **START** is always at the bottom), **LAPS** (**SESSIONS**: every lap, where time went, the race and the career numbers; **VIDEOS**: your lap videos, watch, **Save**, **Share**, and **Make share video**, see below). A bar at the top always shows rig, sim and car; the page's main button sits above the tab bar. Pressing the tab you are on, or swiping back, closes an opened session or video. While you are in the car it only offers what the deck offers then. A phone you no longer use: Home › **Paired phones › Forget**.

## Share a lap, and see it in iRacing

**Share video.** Paddock films your best laps by itself. To send one to a friend or put it on YouTube, open the lap on your iPhone (**LAPS › VIDEOS**, tap the lap) and press **Make share video**. The PC adds the numbers on top (speed, gear, throttle and brake, the lap time running, how far you are ahead or behind your best other lap, a small map with the car on it, your name and number, and a small Paddock mark), in 1080p, the right way up. It takes about a minute and only starts when you are out of the car. When it says **Ready to share**, press **Save** (then find it in Files and share it from there) or **Share** (only where the phone offers its share sheet). On the PC, the same button is in the analysis, on the **VIDEO** page: **MAKE SHARE VIDEO**, then **SHOW SHARE VIDEO** opens the folder with the file selected.

**See it in iRacing.** In the analysis, every event on a lap (ABS, lock-up, wheel slip) and every pass in a race has a small **SEE IN iRACING** button. It takes iRacing's own replay to that moment, three seconds before, on your car (or the car that passed you), playing. The button only shows while iRacing has that same session open and you are out of the car; otherwise it stays hidden.

## What happens by itself

- The PC starts the rig when you log on. Stream Deck comes up first, then the dashboard, the wheel, the haptics, the overlays, the spotter, and the iRacing app.
- iRacing is given the PC first when a session starts.
- Rig Doctor checks the rig at startup and now and then while you are out of the car. It fixes what it can by itself: overlays, the spotter, Stream Deck, and keeping iRacing's files on this PC. The idle screen says "All good" when there is nothing for you to do. It also shows a line for the last session's frame rate. It only asks you about things only you can do, such as a cable.
- Nothing restarts, pops up, or reloads the dashboard while you are driving. A new version of the top screen waits until you are out of the car.
